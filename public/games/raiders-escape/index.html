How to Play

  1. Set your bet, then press Run to start the chase.
  2. The multiplier climbs while she runs from the boulder.
  3. Press Cash Out before the boulder hits to lock in your win and escape.
  4. If the boulder hits first, the round crashes and you lose the stake.
  5. Optional: turn on Auto cash to escape automatically at a chosen multiplier.

Longer you stay, higher the payout — and the risk.
