How To Play

Pick your winning faces. Roll the die. Every roll settles instantly.

Tap any 1 to 5 of the six faces — those are the faces you win on. Fewer faces covered means a lower chance to win, but a bigger multiplier: 1 face covered pays , 5 faces covered pays . Chance and multiplier recalculate live with every tap.

Roll one of your covered faces and you win — your profit is added to your balance the moment the dice stop. Miss, and your bet is already settled; there's nothing more to lose.

You can freely change your bet amount and selected faces any time you're not actively rolling. Just note: changing your selected faces or your bet amount while a win streak is active resets that streak back to 0 — you'll be asked to confirm first.
