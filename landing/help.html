Game & wallet guide

Game credits, explained

The current BGaming catalogue opens with provider demo credits. You do not need to deposit. These credits are separate from your wallet, have no cash value and cannot be withdrawn. Current rounds are not settled on-chain by Tribet.

Any future real-money mode will need its own clear rules and availability information. Do not send funds expecting to wager in the current games.

Wallets & networks

The connection menu offers MetaMask, Base (formerly Coinbase Wallet), and WalletConnect. Compatible installed wallets such as Phantom may also appear, depending on your browser and device.

The production wallet configuration uses ETH on Arbitrum One. Use a wallet with EVM and Arbitrum One support. This does not mean the current games accept ETH or any other coin as payment.

Connecting a wallet is not a payment. Never share a recovery phrase or private key. Review any wallet request before approving it.

Keep play balanced

18+ only, or the legal minimum age in your jurisdiction if higher. Availability varies by region. Treat games as entertainment, take breaks and never gamble with money you cannot afford to lose.

The app offers a configurable reality-check reminder. Saved deposit and loss limits are not currently enforced. Do not rely on these settings as a spending block.
