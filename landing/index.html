Frequently asked questions

What can I play on Tribet?

Explore BGaming slots, casual games, table games and scratch games. The current catalogue uses provider demo credits, with no cash winnings. Sports and prediction markets are not available yet.

Which wallets can I connect?

The connection menu offers MetaMask, Base (formerly Coinbase Wallet), and WalletConnect. Compatible installed wallets, such as Phantom, may also appear. Use an EVM-compatible wallet with Arbitrum One support. Availability depends on your device.

Which coins and networks are supported?

The current wallet configuration uses ETH on Arbitrum One. This is wallet compatibility, not a list of currencies accepted by the current game catalogue. Other casino payment currencies have not been enabled.

Do I need to deposit to try the games?

No. Current BGaming games use provider demo credits, separate from your connected wallet balance. These credits have no cash value and cannot be withdrawn. Do not send funds to play a demo.

Are game results verified on-chain?

The current catalogue opens third-party BGaming games in demo mode. Tribet does not settle these rounds on-chain or claim on-chain verification of their outcomes. Check each game’s own rules and information.

Who can use Tribet?

You must be at least 18 and meet the legal age requirement in your jurisdiction. Availability varies by region. Treat games as entertainment, take breaks and never gamble with money you cannot afford to lose.
